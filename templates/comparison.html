Why the results differ

Greedy treats each day independently and adds a safety margin whenever irrigation is needed. It is simple and fast, but does not consider future days.

Dynamic Programming evaluates skip/water choices across the full period. It maximises safe days, prioritises dry and hot days when the budget is tight, and uses the least water among equally good plans.
